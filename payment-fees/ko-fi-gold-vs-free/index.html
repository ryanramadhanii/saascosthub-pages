Common questions: Ko-fi Creator Shop (Creator Tips & Memberships)

How much does Ko-fi Creator Shop take from $100 in Creator Tips & Memberships?

On a domestic $100 USD transaction, Ko-fi Creator Shop deducts $8.20 (7.9% + $0.30), leaving $91.80 net payout. If the buyer uses an international card requiring currency conversion, total deductions reach $9.70 (9.7%).

How much should I invoice to receive $100 after Ko-fi Creator Shop fees?

To net an exact $100.00 after domestic Ko-fi Creator Shop processing fees, charge $108.90. On cross-border FX transactions, charge $110.71.

Does Ko-fi Creator Shop handle VAT, GST, and US sales tax automatically?

No. Ko-fi Creator Shop is a payment processor, not a Merchant of Record. You remain responsible for tax registration and remittance (automated tax calculation add-ons typically cost an extra 0.5% per transaction).

Why are Ko-fi Creator Shop effective fees higher on $10 micro-orders?

Because the fixed $0.30 per-charge fee applies regardless of cart size. On a $10 order, Ko-fi Creator Shop takes $1.09, resulting in a 10.9% effective deduction.

What are Ko-fi Creator Shop currency conversion exchange rate and dispute fees in Creator Tips & Memberships?

Cross-border cards add +1.5% and currency conversion adds +0% above mid-market exchange rates (totaling 9.40% + $0.30). Standard card chargeback or dispute handling fees typically run $15 to $25 per disputed transaction unless covered by seller protection.
