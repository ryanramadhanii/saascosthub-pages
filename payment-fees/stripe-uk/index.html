Common questions: Stripe UK (United Kingdom)

How much does Stripe UK take from £100 in United Kingdom?

On a domestic £100 GBP transaction, Stripe UK deducts £1.70 (1.5% + £0.20), leaving £98.30 net payout. If the buyer uses an international card requiring currency conversion, total deductions reach £5.45 (5.45%).

How much should I invoice to receive £100 after Stripe UK fees?

To net an exact £100.00 after domestic Stripe UK processing fees, charge £101.73. On cross-border FX transactions, charge £105.75.

Does Stripe UK handle VAT, GST, and US sales tax automatically?

No. Stripe UK is a payment processor, not a Merchant of Record. You remain responsible for tax registration and remittance (automated tax calculation add-ons typically cost an extra 0.5% per transaction).

Why are Stripe UK effective fees higher on £10 micro-orders?

Because the fixed £0.20 per-charge fee applies regardless of cart size. On a £10 order, Stripe UK takes £0.35, resulting in a 3.5% effective deduction.

What are Stripe UK currency conversion exchange rate and dispute fees in United Kingdom?

Cross-border cards add +1.75% and currency conversion adds +2% above mid-market exchange rates (totaling 5.25% + £0.20). Standard card chargeback or dispute handling fees typically run £15 to £25 per disputed transaction unless covered by seller protection.
