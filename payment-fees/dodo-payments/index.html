Common questions: Dodo Payments (Global Merchant of Record)

How much does Dodo Payments take from $100 in Global Merchant of Record?

On a domestic $100 USD transaction, Dodo Payments deducts $4.40 (4% + $0.40), leaving $95.60 net payout. If the buyer uses an international card requiring currency conversion, total deductions reach $4.40 (4.4%).

How much should I invoice to receive $100 after Dodo Payments fees?

To net an exact $100.00 after domestic Dodo Payments processing fees, charge $104.58. On cross-border FX transactions, charge $104.58.

Does Dodo Payments handle VAT, GST, and US sales tax automatically?

Yes. Dodo Payments operates as a Merchant of Record, meaning global VAT, GST, and US state sales tax collection and filing are bundled into its 4% + $0.40 fee.

Why are Dodo Payments effective fees higher on $10 micro-orders?

Because the fixed $0.40 per-charge fee applies regardless of cart size. On a $10 order, Dodo Payments takes $0.80, resulting in a 8% effective deduction.
