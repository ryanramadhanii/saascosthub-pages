Sendinblue (Now Brevo) Pricing Breakdown: Send-Volume Tiers vs Contact Limits

Sendinblue rebranded to Brevo and bills by monthly email volume instead of contact count. Here is what 5,000 to 100,000 monthly emails actually cost.

How Sendinblue’s rebrand to Brevo changed free and Starter limits

Thousands of founders still search for Sendinblue pricing when auditing email marketing costs. Since rebranding to Brevo, the core pricing model remains anchored on monthly emails sent rather than stored contacts, with one major catch on the free plan.

Brevo Free stores up to 100,000 contacts at $0/month, but enforces a hard ceiling of 300 emails per day. If your list has 1,200 subscribers, you cannot send a single weekly newsletter broadcast without upgrading to a paid Starter quota.

  • Brevo Free ($0/mo): Unlimited/100k stored contacts, capped at 300 emails per day with Brevo branding.
  • Brevo Starter ($9/mo base): Starts at 5,000 emails/month; removing the Brevo logo adds an extra $12/month add-on.
  • Brevo Business ($18/mo base): Unlocks multi-step marketing automation, A/B testing, and send-time optimization.

Common questions

Is Sendinblue the exact same company as Brevo?

Yes. Sendinblue officially rebranded to Brevo in May 2023. Existing Sendinblue accounts, SMTP relays, and API keys transitioned under the Brevo domain.

How can I send weekly newsletters to 5,000 subscribers for $0/month?

Because Brevo Free caps daily sends at 300 emails/day, weekly senders with 1,000 to 10,000 subscribers can use Kit’s free Newsletter plan instead, which allows unlimited broadcast sends to up to 10,000 subscribers.
