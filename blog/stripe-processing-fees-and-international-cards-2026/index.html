Stripe Processing Fees & International Card Costs: Complete 2026 Breakdown

US domestic cards cost 2.9% + $0.30, yet cross-border cards, currency conversion, and recurring billing add-ons quietly push real Stripe deductions between 4.4% and 6.6%.

Every layer inside a 2026 US Stripe card charge

When business owners look up Stripe processing fees, the 2.9% + $0.30 domestic card rate is only the starting point. Stripe prices add-on capabilities modularly, meaning each cross-border card or recurring invoice adds separate percentage points on top of the base rate.

On a $1,000 invoice paid by a domestic US Visa card without recurring software add-ons, Stripe deducts $29.30 and settles $970.70. If that exact same $1,000 invoice is paid by a Canadian or British customer in their local currency, total deductions climb to $54.30 (leaving $945.70).

How to calculate Stripe fees in reverse so you net an exact amount

A frequent billing mistake is adding 2.9% + $0.30 onto a $1,000 invoice ($1,029.30). Because Stripe takes 2.9% of the entire gross charge ($1,029.30 * 0.029 + $0.30 = $30.15), you still come up $0.85 short ($999.15 net).

Divide `(TargetNet + FixedFee)` by `(1 - Rate)` instead. For domestic US cards (`r = 0.029`, `f = $0.30`), billing `($1,000 + $0.30) / 0.971 = $1,030.18` nets an exact $1,000.00. For cross-border FX cards (`r = 0.054`), bill `($1,000 + $0.30) / 0.946 = $1,057.40`.

Common questions

How much are Stripe processing fees on $100?

On a standard domestic US $100 card charge (2.9% + $0.30), Stripe deducts $3.20 and pays out $96.80. On a non-US card with currency conversion (5.4% + $0.30), Stripe deducts $5.70 and pays out $94.30.

Does Stripe refund the 2.9% + $0.30 fee when you refund a customer?

No. Under standard Stripe US pricing, processing fees from the original charge are not returned to the merchant when issuing a partial or full customer refund.
