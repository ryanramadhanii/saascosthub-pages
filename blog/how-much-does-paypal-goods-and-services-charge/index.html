How Much Does PayPal Goods and Services Charge in 2026?

Standard US Goods & Services transfers take 2.99% with zero fixed cent fee, whereas formal PayPal Invoicing jumps to 3.49% + $0.49 (and nearly 9% across borders).

Why two sellers see two different PayPal fees on the exact same sale

PayPal splits commercial buyer-protected payments into two separate rate tiers in the United States. When a buyer sends money directly to your email or @username and selects "Paying for an item or service" (Goods & Services), PayPal charges a flat 2.99% with $0.00 fixed fee on domestic USD transfers.

When you generate a formal PayPal Invoice or use a PayPal Checkout button on a website, PayPal applies its Commercial Checkout schedule of 3.49% + $0.49 per transaction. On a $50 item, standard G&S deducts $1.50 ($48.50 net), while a PayPal Invoice deducts $2.24 ($47.76 net).

What happens when a Goods & Services buyer is outside your country

Cross-border Goods & Services payments stack two surcharges onto the 2.99% base rate: a 1.50% international commercial transaction fee (bringing the processing cut to 4.49%), plus a 3.0% to 4.0% currency conversion spread whenever PayPal converts USD, GBP, EUR, or CAD into your local balance.

On a $1,000 overseas freelance payment converted to local currency, ~8.49% ($84.90) disappears before the money hits your bank. Freelancers working with repeat verified clients eliminate both surcharges by invoicing via local multi-currency bank details on Wise Business (~0.48% mid-market FX).

Common questions

How much should a buyer send via PayPal Goods and Services so I get $100?

For a domestic US Goods & Services payment (2.99% fee), divide $100 by (1 - 0.0299) = $103.08. When the buyer sends $103.08, PayPal deducts $3.08 and leaves an exact $100.00 in your balance.

Can business sellers use Friends & Family to avoid the 2.99% fee?

No. PayPal prohibits commercial sales over Friends & Family, voids both Seller Chargeback Protection and Buyer Protection, and restricts US business accounts from receiving domestic Friends & Family transfers.
