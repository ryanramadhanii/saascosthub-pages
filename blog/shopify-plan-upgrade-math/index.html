The Exact Monthly Revenue Where Upgrading Your Shopify Plan Saves Money

Upgrading from Shopify Basic ($39) to Grow ($105) loses money below $33,000/month on Shopify Payments, yet saves money at just $6,600/month if you use an external gateway.

Why order count cancels out on Shopify Payments upgrades

Every US Shopify plan charges the exact same $0.30 fixed fee per online credit card transaction. Whether you process 100 orders of $300 or 1,000 orders of $30, the fixed cent portion stays identical across Basic, Grow, and Advanced.

That means upgrading plans is a pure trade between higher fixed monthly subscription fees and lower percentage card rates.

  • Shopify Basic ($39/mo monthly, $29/mo annual): 2.9% + $0.30 online US cards.
  • Shopify Grow ($105/mo monthly, $79/mo annual): 2.7% + $0.30 online US cards.
  • Shopify Advanced ($399/mo monthly, $299/mo annual): 2.5% + $0.30 online US cards.

Exact monthly GMV crossover table (Shopify Payments vs external gateway)

On monthly billing with Shopify Payments, Grow costs $66/month more than Basic and trims 0.2% (0.002) off card volume: $66 divided by 0.002 equals $33,000/month in card sales.

If your store operates in a region without Shopify Payments and uses a third-party gateway, Shopify adds a 2.0% surcharge on Basic, 1.0% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced. That 1.0% surcharge drop cuts the upgrade break-even fivefold.

What to do if you are stuck paying the 2% external gateway penalty

At $25,000/month in store sales outside Shopify Payments countries, Shopify Basic charges $500/month in pure platform surcharges on top of your local payment processor. Upgrading to Grow cuts that penalty to $250/month, while self-hosted WooCommerce or BigCommerce charge 0% extra gateway fees.

Common questions

Does annual Shopify billing lower the upgrade break-even point?

Yes. Annual billing lowers the Basic-to-Grow gap from $66/mo ($105 minus $39) to $50/mo ($79 minus $29), dropping the native Shopify Payments crossover from $33,000/mo down to $25,000/mo.

Do PayPal orders on a US Shopify store incur the 2% third-party fee?

Not if Shopify Payments is active as your primary gateway on the store. Shopify waives the third-party transaction surcharge on PayPal Express orders when Shopify Payments is enabled.
