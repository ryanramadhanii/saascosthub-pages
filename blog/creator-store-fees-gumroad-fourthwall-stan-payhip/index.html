Mapping creator checkouts from $0/month to $5,000/month in digital sales

Digital template, course, and ebook creators usually start on Gumroad because setup takes five minutes and costs $0/month upfront. What stings is Gumroad’s 10% + $0.50 platform cut stacked on top of 2.9% + $0.30 card processing (~12.9% + $0.80 per order, or 30% on Gumroad Discover).

Before jumping straight to a $29/month subscription store, it helps to compare intermediate 4% to 5% zero-monthly-fee options alongside flat-rate checkouts.

Three exact monthly break-even thresholds

Because underlying 2.9% + $0.30 Stripe fees apply on almost every non-MoR creator platform, you can calculate exact upgrade points directly from the platform cut:

  • Ko-fi Free (5%) -> Ko-fi Gold ($12/mo, 0%): flips at exactly $240/month in tips and shop sales.
  • Gumroad (10%) -> Stan Store ($29/mo, 0%): flips below $290/month in digital sales.
  • Fourthwall Free or Payhip Free (5%) -> Stan Store ($29/mo, 0%): flips at exactly $580/month in digital sales.
  • Payhip Free (5%) -> Payhip Plus ($29/mo, 2%): flips at $967/month (and still loses to Stan Store 0% at the same $29/mo bill).

Common questions

What is the cheapest way to sell digital downloads while building an email list?

Kit Commerce on the free Newsletter plan (up to 10,000 subscribers) charges 3.5% + $0.30 all-in including Stripe processing with $0 monthly subscription fee, undercutting both Gumroad (~12.9% + $0.80) and 5% creator storefronts while tagging buyers directly in your email list.

Does Fourthwall charge 5% on physical merch too?

No. Fourthwall charges 0% platform fee on physical print-on-demand catalog merch (building margin into base unit production costs) and applies the 5% fee only to digital downloads and memberships on its free plan.
