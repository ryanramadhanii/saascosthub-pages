When Substack’s 10% Cut Costs More Than Flat-Fee Newsletters

On a $10/month newsletter, Substack plus Stripe deducts $1.66 per renewal (16.6%). Here is the exact paid reader count where flat-tier platforms pay for themselves.

Jump to live calculator ↓ Compare Kit Free & Creator Plans →

The three fees deducted from every $10 Substack renewal

Starting a publication on Substack carries zero fixed overhead while your list is free. That makes it low-risk for testing a new topic and tapping into Substack Notes recommendations.

Once you turn on paid subscriptions, however, three separate deductions come out of every reader payment before cash hits your bank account.

  • Substack platform commission: 10.0% ($1.00 on a $10/mo subscription).
  • Stripe credit card processing: 2.9% + $0.30 ($0.59 on a $10/mo charge).
  • Stripe recurring billing fee passed through to writers: 0.7% ($0.07 on $10/mo).

Net payout per $10/month subscriber

Out of every $10.00 monthly reader charge on Substack, total fees take $1.66 (16.6% effective take-rate), leaving $8.34 net.

Break-even subscriber counts across Kit, beehiiv, Buttondown, and Ghost

Flat-tier newsletter platforms charge a predictable monthly software fee (based on total email list size) and take 0% to 3.5% on paid memberships. Below ~30 to 50 paying members, Substack is cheaper. Past 100 paying members, percentage cuts compound fast.

Keeping Substack discovery without surrendering 10% at scale

Many writers who cross $1,000/month in reader subscriptions keep a free Substack profile active solely for Notes discovery while hosting their primary email list, automations, and paid products on Kit (which includes a free Newsletter tier up to 10,000 subscribers) or beehiiv.

Common questions

Can you export your paid subscribers if you leave Substack later?

Yes. Substack lets writers export their full free email list as CSV and disconnect or migrate active Stripe subscriptions to another Stripe-connected platform such as Kit, Ghost, or beehiiv without forcing readers to re-enter credit card numbers.

Does Kit charge a monthly fee under 10,000 subscribers?

Kit offers a free Newsletter plan up to 10,000 subscribers with basic broadcasts and paid digital product/subscription checkouts (3.5% + $0.30 commerce fee), while multi-step visual automations require the paid Creator plan.
