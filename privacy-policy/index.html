Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Calculator inputs remain in your browser

All revenue figures, order counts, and subscriber numbers entered into SaaS Cost Hub calculators are processed locally in your browser via client-side JavaScript. We do not store or transmit calculator inputs to any database.

2. Log files, analytics & contact submissions

Like standard static websites hosted on content delivery networks, server logs record basic non-identifying request metadata (browser type, referring page, timestamp). If you submit our contact form, your name, email address, and message are stored solely to respond to your inquiry.

3. Google AdSense & advertising cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to SaaS Cost Hub and/or other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or www.aboutads.info.

4. Affiliate partner links

When you click outbound partner links to software providers, the destination vendor or affiliate network (such as PartnerStack or Impact) may set an attribution cookie on their domain to credit referrals in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Contact regarding privacy

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, contact us at hello@saascosthub.com or via our contact page.
