Common questions

Which option is cheapest at $100,000/month in online sales?

At $100,000/month ($100 average order value), Shopify Grow (Standard) has the lowest combined subscription + transaction cost at $3105.00/month (3.1% effective take-rate), saving $1476/year versus WooCommerce (Cloudways VPS).

Does billing annually change the e-commerce plan break-even point?

Yes. Annual Shopify and BigCommerce plans cut fixed monthly subscription fees by roughly 25%, pulling upgrade break-even thresholds noticeably lower—use the Annual Billing toggle in the calculator above to test exact numbers.

How do third-party payment gateway fees affect store profit?

Using an external gateway on Shopify adds 2.0% on Basic, 1.0% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced on top of your card processor fee, whereas WooCommerce and BigCommerce add 0% gateway surcharges.
