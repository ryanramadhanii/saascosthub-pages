Common questions

What is the cheapest email platform here at 10,000 subscribers?

At 10,000 contacts (100 paid members at $10/mo), MailerLite costs $73.00/month compared to $239.00/month on ActiveCampaign—saving $1992/year.

Why do some email tools bill for unsubscribed contacts?

Audience-based tools like Mailchimp and Klaviyo count active or unarchived profiles toward your tier bracket, whereas send-volume tools like Brevo store contacts free and bill only for monthly emails sent.

When does Substack 10% become more expensive than beehiiv or Ghost?

Once gross paid subscription revenue crosses ~$490/month (49 subscribers paying $10/mo), Substack's 10% platform fee exceeds a flat $49/month beehiiv Scale subscription—and the gap widens with every new paid reader.
